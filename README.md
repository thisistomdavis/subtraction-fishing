# 🎣 Subtraction Fishing! - Column Subtraction Game

A vibrant, kid-friendly educational game designed to help children practice 2 and 3-digit columnar subtraction with "exchanging" (borrowing).

![Game Preview](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Complete-brightgreen)
![Tech](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tech-HTML%2FCSS%2FJS-blue)
![Built with Gemini](https://img.shields.io/badge/Built%20with-Gemini-8e44ad)

## ✨ Features

- **Fishing Pond Interface:** Cast your line into a natural pond to solve subtraction problems.
- **Smart Problem Generation:** Generates valid subtraction challenges between two and three digits that never result in a negative number.
- **Interactive Borrowing UI:**
  - **Cross Out:** Click any digit in the top row to cross it out when borrowing.
  - **Replacement Values:** Type the new value in dashed boxes above the crossed-out digit.
  - **Move a 1:** Click the faint area next to a digit to "move the 1" over, helping to track the exchange.
- **Celebration & Junk:** 
  - **Correct Answers:** Catch a variety of colorful fish (🐟, 🐠, 🐋) with a splash of water!
  - **Incorrect Answers:** Catch "junk" like old boots (🥾) or tin cans (🥫).
- **Printable Reports:** Generate a clean report showing the subtraction grid and a summary of the challenge.

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **New Fish:** Click **NEW FISH! 🐟** to generate a new subtraction problem.
2. **Solve the Problem:** Calculate the difference between the two numbers.
3. **Use the Borrowing Tools:**
   - Click a digit to cross it out.
   - Type the new value in the box above it.
   - Click the space next to the next digit to show the borrowed "1".
4. **Enter Your Answer:** Type each digit of your result into the yellow boxes.
5. **Cast Your Line:** Click **CAST LINE! 🎣** to check your answer. If you're right, see what you've caught!
